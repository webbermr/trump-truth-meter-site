Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “Iran has lost control of the Strait of Hormuz.”
    September 2026 reporting shows more crude moving on a southern, U.S.-guided lane near Oman, and some analysts (including Kpler, cited by CNN, and Stahel) describe Iran as losing influence over shipping. That is not the same as losing control of the strait: transit volumes remain far below prewar levels (roughly 10 million barrels a day versus about 17–20 million, or less than a third of February flows in some tallies), Iran continues to threaten or disrupt normal traffic, and no government has taken operational control of the waterway.

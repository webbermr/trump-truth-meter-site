Claims checked

  1. True
    “Greg Cunningham is running for U.S. Representative from New Mexico’s 2nd Congressional District against Democrat Gabe Vasquez.”
    Cunningham is the 2026 Republican nominee challenging Democratic incumbent Gabe Vasquez in NM-02. [1][2]
  2. Mostly true
    “Cunningham is a retired United States Marine who served in combat.”
    He is a retired Marine (about 12–13 years), including Force Recon, with deployments that included Somalia and a Marine Expeditionary Unit. Campaign and news accounts call him a combat veteran; public bios do not name a specific combat tour beyond those deployments. [2][3][4]
  3. True
    “He then served over 20 years in the Albuquerque Police Department.”
    He joined APD around 2004 and retired in 2025 after about 21 years, as a detective and later a lieutenant. [2][3][4]
  4. Misleading
    “Gabe Vasquez supports defunding the police.”
    In 2020, before Congress, Vasquez wrote that some police funding should be redirected to social programs and said “defund the police” was not the right term for what he supported. In the House he has voted for law-enforcement funding, backed the Invest to Protect Act, and highlighted police grants. Treating 2020 comments as his current stance omits that record. [5][6][7]
  5. Misleading
    “Gabe Vasquez supports a $5 trillion tax hike.”
    The figure comes from CBO estimates that extending the 2017 tax cuts, or the 2025 tax law Democrats opposed, reduces federal revenue by roughly $4–4.5 trillion over ten years. Voting against that extension is not the same as proposing a new $5 trillion tax increase. [8][9]
    🔁 Rated misleading 2 times before, since Oct 3.
  6. Misleading
    “Gabe Vasquez supports destroying New Mexico’s oil and gas industry, which would make state taxes soar and school budgets collapse.”
    Oil and gas is a large share of New Mexico’s general fund and school revenue, so a real shutdown would blow a major hole in the budget. Vasquez has backed leasing reforms, methane rules, and limits on some new public-land drilling, while saying existing production should continue and that the industry remains important to the state. He has not proposed ending it. [10][11][12]
  7. False
    “The United States is bigger than any country in the world in energy dominance, times three.”
    The U.S. is the world’s largest crude-oil and natural-gas producer. In 2025 it produced about 13.6 million barrels of crude a day, roughly 40% more than Russia or Saudi Arabia (about 9.6–9.9 million), not three times as much. No standard energy metric shows a threefold lead. [13][14]
  8. Opinion
    “In Congress, Cunningham will vote for strong borders, energy dominance, low taxes, and the Second Amendment, and will never let voters down.”
    These are predictions and campaign praise, not checkable outcomes.

    “High prices are why he won the election.”

  4. False
    “Prices are now coming down rapidly.”
    CPI rose 0.4% in August 2026 after 0.1% in July, and was up 3.4% from a year earlier. Energy was up 16.3% over the year. The national gas average was $4.41 on Oct. 1, 2026, versus about $3.16 a year earlier, even after a small weekly dip. September CPI was not yet released. [1][3]
  5. Opinion
    “Current high prices are the Democrats' fault, not the Republicans', and Republicans are fixing them.”
    Assigning partisan blame and claiming the administration is fixing prices are political judgments, not checkable facts. The measured price level has risen since January 2025.

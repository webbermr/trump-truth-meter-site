Claims checked

  1. False
    “Prices are way down from what Biden and the Democrats left.”
    The not-seasonally-adjusted CPI-U rose from 317.671 in January 2025 to 334.980 in August 2026, about 5.4% higher, not lower. August 2026 was the latest CPI available on October 1; September’s release was scheduled for October 14. [1][2][3]
  2. False
    “Prices are now coming down rapidly.”
    CPI rose 0.4% in August 2026 after 0.1% in July, and 3.4% over the prior 12 months. A 0.4% drop in June did not reverse the higher price level. That is ongoing inflation, not rapid deflation, and the 12-month rate was above the 3.0% rate in January 2025. [1][2]
  3. True
    “Trump won the Election.”
    Donald Trump won the November 2024 presidential election, with 312 electoral votes to Kamala Harris’s 226. [4]
    🔁 Checked once before.
    • Also checked by professional fact-checkers:
    • Snopes.com: True on “America.gov — an artificial intelligence chatbot the U.S. government launched in September 2026 — stated that U.S. President Donald Trump lost the 2020 presidential election, contradicting his longstanding claim that he won three elections.”
  4. Mostly true
    “High prices are why he won the election.”
    Inflation was a major voting issue: AP VoteCast found voters who named inflation as their most important factor were almost twice as likely to support Trump as Harris, and about half of Trump voters cited high prices as their biggest issue. That supports prices as an important reason, not the sole cause. [5][6]
  5. Opinion
    “High prices are the Democrats’ fault, not the Republicans’, and Republicans are fixing them.”
    Assigning exclusive partisan blame and credit is political judgment. The factual assertion that prices are being brought down is already rated false above.

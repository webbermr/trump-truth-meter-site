Claims checked

  1. False
    “CNN and MSDNC refused to cover or be at Xi Jinping's White House arrival because they didn't want Trump to get credit”
    CNN, MS NOW, Deadline, the Washington Post and Yahoo all report that the White House turned away CNN and MS NOW journalists at the North Portico arrival. This happened hours after a federal judge ordered their press access temporarily restored. Nothing supports the motive Trump gives.
  2. True
    “Xi Jinping and Peng Liyuan arrived at the White House, with a State Dinner that night”
    Xi's state visit included a White House arrival and a State Dinner on September 24, 2026.
  3. Mostly true
    “Won in a giant landslide: all 7 swing states, the popular vote and 86% of counties”
    In 2024 Trump won all seven swing states, a popular-vote plurality (49.8% to 48.3%) and about 85–87% of counties. A 1.5-point margin and 312 electoral votes are not a 'giant landslide', and counties vary hugely in population.
  4. Misleading
    “CNN and MSDNC ratings have dropped considerably because of no credibility”
    Nielsen figures show weekly and month-to-month swings. For example, CNN's primetime audience fell 5% from July to August, while the networks reported gains over the year before. That is not a clear 'considerable' drop, and the cause he gives is opinion.
  5. Misleading
    “His popularity has grown because of unprecedented success”
    This is mostly self-praise, but a mid-September Economist/YouGov poll put his approval at 35% and disapproval at 62%. That does not support the claim of growing popularity.

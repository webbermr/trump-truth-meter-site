Claims checked

  1. True
    “Bayer just announced a new $2.2 billion pharmaceutical manufacturing campus near Columbus, Ohio.”
    On October 2, 2026, Bayer announced a planned $2.2 billion manufacturing site in New Albany, in the Columbus area. It is a multi-year plan, not a plant already built. [1][2]
  2. Mostly true
    “The campus will generate thousands of jobs for Ohioans in advanced manufacturing, scientific research, construction, and more.”
    Officials and reporting put the total near 2,000–2,500 positions when temporary construction jobs are included. Permanent high-skilled roles are in the hundreds, not thousands, and operations are years away. [1][2][3]
    🔁 Checked once before.
  3. True
    “Trump was heading to Ohio when the post was published.”
    The White House schedule and contemporaneous reports placed him in Ohio on October 3, 2026, including an event tied to the Bayer announcement. [4][5]
  4. Misleading
    “The investment is thanks to Trump's pharmaceutical tariffs, which have led to over $600 billion in new pharma investments.”
    The White House and industry tallies cite more than $600 billion in announced pharmaceutical manufacturing commitments, not money already spent, and not all of it is caused by tariffs. Bayer has also cited longer-term U.S. expansion plans, workforce, and other policy pressure. [6][7][8]
    🔁 Rated misleading once before (Sep 26).
  5. Misleading
    “Those policies have produced lower Most Favored Nation (MFN) drug prices.”
    The administration has announced MFN-style deals covering selected drugs and channels, many not fully in effect. That is not a broad, already-realized drop in U.S. drug prices. [9][10]
    🔁 Rated false once before (Oct 1).

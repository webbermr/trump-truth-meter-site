  3. Mostly true

  4. False
    “A few years ago Michigan, Wisconsin, and Pennsylvania were not even competitive for Republicans, and Trump put them back in play.”
    Democrats carried all three from 1992 through 2012, and Trump did flip them in 2016, but they were already competitive. Bush lost Wisconsin by about 0.2 points in 2000 and 0.4 points in 2004, Pennsylvania by about 2.5 points in 2004, and Michigan by about 3.4 points in 2004. Even the wider Obama-era margins (Pennsylvania about 5 points in 2012) do not support “not even competitive,” and all three stayed among the closest states in 2016, 2020, and 2024. [4][5][6][7]

    “Trump won those states by winning working-class voters.”

  6. Opinion

  7. Opinion

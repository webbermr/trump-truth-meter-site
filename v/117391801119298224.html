Claims checked

  1. Mostly true
    “Becton Dickinson (misspelled “Beckton Dickenson”) is one of the largest medical device manufacturers and agreed to invest $3 billion to expand U.S. manufacturing of essential medical products such as syringes, needles, and lab kits.”
    BD is a major medical-technology company. Its Oct. 6, 2026 announcement commits $3 billion toward expanding U.S. manufacturing of essential medical consumables, including making BD needles used in America domestically. Syringes fit that program; “lab kits” are not specified. The $3 billion is part of a larger multiyear U.S. commitment reported at about $19 billion, not the whole deal. [1][2]
  2. Misleading
    “The biggest expansion will be in Nebraska, where BD will invest over $1 billion, including needles made with American steel.”
    BD says it will make needles used in America in the U.S. using American-made steel. Its public announcement does not allocate more than $1 billion to Nebraska or call Nebraska the largest share of this expansion. Earlier disclosed Nebraska projects, including a $110 million Columbus expansion, are far smaller and do not establish the new figure. [1][2][3]
  3. False
    “Trump was heading to Nebraska when the post went up.”
    The post is timestamped 03:18 GMT on Oct. 6, which was late evening Central time on Oct. 5. By then he had already arrived in Grand Island and spoken at the rally, so “heading there today” was no longer accurate. [4][5]
    🔁 Checked once before.
  4. Misleading
    “BD announced the investment because of Trump’s medical-device tariffs, which will be in place by the end of the year.”
    BD’s deal offers conditional relief from possible future Section 232 medical-device tariffs in exchange for U.S. commitments, so tariff policy is part of the bargain. Those tariffs are not in force; rates, product scope, and timing were still undetermined. Saying they will be in place by year-end is a prediction, not a completed policy, and BD does not say tariffs alone caused an investment it had been building toward. [1][2][6]

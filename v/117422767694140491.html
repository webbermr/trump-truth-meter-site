Claims checked

  1. False
    “Radical-left Democrats, socialists, and communists rampaged through cities and towns and ripped down almost all Christopher Columbus statues.”
    A 2021 Washington Post and MIT count found at least 40 Columbus monuments removed since 2018, against more than 130 that still stood — a minority, not almost all. The New York Times later put the 2020 wave at more than 30 toppled or taken down. Newsweek counted at least seven destroyed by protesters and at least 27 ordered removed by city officials, so many came down by government decision, not a nationwide mob of socialists and communists. [1][2][3]
  2. False
    “Those days of plunder are over, and the removed Columbus statues are back, more magnificent than ever.”
    Some damaged statues were repaired and moved to less public places, such as Italian-American clubs or church grounds, and a replica of Baltimore’s harbor-dumped statue was installed near the White House in March 2026. That is not a general return. The City Hall statue in Columbus, Ohio, was still in storage this weekend, and on Oct. 9 Trump was still demanding it be put back; other well-known removals, including San Francisco and Chicago’s Grant Park statue, have not been restored to their old sites. “More magnificent than ever” is a boast. [2][4][5][6]
  3. Opinion
    “This may be the biggest day ever for Italian Americans and for Italy.”
    Holiday superlative, not a checkable fact. The greeting itself does not assert that Oct. 11 is the federal holiday; that falls on Monday, Oct. 12, 2026.

Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “Joe Scarborough's show has been cut way back.”
    MS NOW did cut Morning Joe from four hours to three starting June 1, 2026, and moved the 9 a.m. hour to other programming. That is a real one-hour trim of a still-daily show, not a drastic cutback. The network said Scarborough and Mika Brzezinski requested the shorter schedule because of the grind, and they had just renewed their contracts in March 2026. [1][2][3]
  2. Opinion
    “Joe Scarborough's show will soon be "dead."”
    This is a prediction, not a reported cancellation. Public reporting describes a requested schedule change and new contracts, not an imminent end to the show. [1][3]
  3. Opinion
    “The show is too predictable and boring.”
    This is a subjective judgment, not a checkable fact.
  4. Misleading
    “Morning Joe has really bad ratings.”
    "Really bad" is partly a judgment, but the implied collapse is not what the numbers show. In April 2026 Morning Joe averaged about 852,000 total viewers, its best month since the 2024 election. In August 2026 the show averaged 733,000 viewers, up 27% from August 2025, per MS NOW's Nielsen figures. It still trails Fox & Friends in total viewers, which is longstanding, not evidence the show is failing. [4][5][6]
  5. Misleading
    “The rest of MS NOW (formerly MSNBC) also has really bad ratings.”
    MSNBC rebranded as MS NOW in November 2025 after the Versant spinoff, so the name is right and "MSDNC" is a nickname. MS NOW still trails Fox News in total audience, but August 2026 Nielsen figures released by the network showed double-digit year-over-year gains across dayparts, including total-day viewership up 28% to 573,000. That is not a network in ratings collapse. [5][7]

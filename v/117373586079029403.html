Claims checked

  1. True
    “Trump appeared at a Republican campaign rally at the Choctaw Event Center in Durant, Oklahoma, on Thursday, Oct. 1, 2026.”
    The Oklahoman, KXII, and the Choctaw Nation reported that President Trump spoke at a capacity rally at the Choctaw Event Center in Durant on Oct. 1, 2026, the first of a planned series of midterm events. [1][2][3]
  2. False
    “The attached page is a Tulsa World article by Randy Krehbiel with photos by Mike Simons.”
    No such Tulsa World story exists. Randy Krehbiel is a real Tulsa World political reporter, and Mike Simons is a real Tulsa World photographer, but the paper’s site and news databases have no Durant-rally article under this headline, byline, or photo credit. [4][5]
  3. False
    “Kevin Hern is the Republican nominee for U.S. Senate in an Oct. 3, 2026 general election.”
    The 2026 general election is Tuesday, Nov. 3, not Oct. 3. Oklahoma’s Senate race is between Republican incumbent Markwayne Mullin and Democrat Troy Green; Hern, who represents the 1st District, is running for re-election to the House, not the Senate. [6][7][8]
  4. False
    “Markwayne Mullin is Homeland Security secretary.”
    Mullin is the junior U.S. senator from Oklahoma and the 2026 Republican Senate nominee. Kristi Noem is the Secretary of Homeland Security. [7][9]

  5. Misleading
    “About 5,000 supporters waited hours in the rain to attend.”
    The venue was filled to its roughly 5,000-person capacity and people lined up early, but reporting describes sun and 90-degree heat, not hours waiting in the rain. [1][2]
  6. Unverifiable
    “Trump said that if Republicans win he will send every American $5,000, and that only he and a Republican Congress can protect “real Americans” from “non-Christian invaders.””
    Those lines appear only on the attached page. Coverage of the Durant speech describes border, economy, and midterm themes and does not report either remark; they cannot be confirmed from the clipping alone. [1][2]

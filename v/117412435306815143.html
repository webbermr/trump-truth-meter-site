Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “Trump just concluded a discussion with Putin in which Russia agreed to supply over 300,000 tons of diesel immediately, 500,000 tons in November, 1,000,000 tons immediately thereafter, and then 3,000,000 tons depending on refinery condition, including to the American marketplace.”
    No White House readout, Kremlin statement, or wire confirmation of this call or these exact volumes was available at posting. Even if discussed, Russian-origin petroleum products remain barred from U.S. import, and Russia had extended a producer diesel-export ban through October 31 amid refinery damage from Ukrainian strikes. The post presents the volumes as an agreed, imminent supply to American and global markets without noting those constraints. [1][2][3][4]
    🔁 Checked once before.
  2. Misleading
    “The United States has total control of the Strait of Hormuz.”
    U.S. officials have claimed Iran lost control and that principal lanes are being kept open, but the strait is an international waterway bordering Iran and Oman. Iran has continued to claim the strait is closed and has attacked or threatened shipping, including a reported tanker strike on October 9. That is not total U.S. control, and earlier claims of record daily barrels flowing freely were false. [5][6][7]
    🔁 Rated false 2 times before, since Sep 29.
  3. Misleading
    “Because of Hormuz control and the Russian diesel announcement, diesel prices for Americans and the world are coming down in record numbers and fast.”
    The 'will be coming down' line is partly a prediction, but 'COMING DOWN, IN RECORD NUMBERS, AND FAST' presents a plunge as underway. The latest EIA weekly reading before the post was about $6.20 a gallon on October 5, down from about $6.38 the week before, still near 2026 highs around $6.50 and far from a record collapse. No verified Russian surge had reached the market. [8][9]
    🔁 Rated false 3 times before, since Sep 29.
  4. Opinion
    “Iran will not have a nuclear weapon.”
    As written, this is a future pledge, not a statement that the program has already been eliminated. Related earlier claims that the nuclear threat was already eliminated were false; the IAEA has said it lacks continuity of knowledge of Iran's enriched-uranium stock. [10]
    🔁 Rated false once before (Sep 29).

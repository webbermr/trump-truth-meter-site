Claims checked

  1. Mostly true
    “ACA Marketplace plan selections rose from about 12 million in 2021, described as the first year of the Biden administration, to about 24 million in 2025.”
    CMS reported about 12.0 million plan selections for 2021 coverage and 24.2 million for 2025, which CMS itself called more than double the 2021 figure. These are plan selections, not a year-round headcount of fraudulent enrollees, and the 2021 open-enrollment period largely occurred before Biden took office. [1][2]
  2. Misleading
    “The Biden administration created a government brokerage program that paid brokers about $25 for each Obamacare enrollment while massively relaxing eligibility checks.”
    The federal Marketplace does not pay broker commissions or set their rates; insurers do, often on a per-member-per-month basis that can be in the tens of dollars, not a flat government bounty of $25 a head. Biden-era enhanced subsidies and weaker consent checks did make improper broker enrollments easier, which CMS itself began cracking down on in 2024. [3][4][5]
  3. Misleading
    “Brokers enrolled people without their knowledge, including by misusing identities, and many of those enrollees never sought care, which shows the enrollment doubling was fraud.”
    GAO and CMS have documented substantial unauthorized enrollments and plan switches, including CMS actions in 2026 against hundreds of thousands of allegedly unauthorized enrollments. Holding coverage without visiting a doctor is not itself proof of fraud, and the documented improper enrollments do not establish that the entire roughly 12-million increase was phantom coverage. Expanded premium subsidies were a major driver of legitimate growth. [2][4][5][6]
  4. Misleading
    “Joe Biden allowed millions upon millions of undocumented immigrants into the country, and that inflow explains why Obamacare rolls doubled.”
    Border encounters were historically high under Biden, but encounters are not the same as tens of millions of people newly eligible for subsidized Marketplace coverage, and the video offers no evidence that those arrivals produced the enrollment increase. Undocumented immigrants generally cannot enroll in Marketplace plans or receive premium tax credits. [6][7]
    🔁 Rated false once before (Oct 3).
  5. Misleading
    “Trump is giving people in Ohio and 30 other states $500 back because Biden overcharged them through Obamacare fraud, and only his administration uncovered and is reversing that theft.”
    The White House said it would send $500 checks to nearly 1 million unsubsidized enrollees in 30 federal-exchange states, including Ohio, funded from a surplus of Marketplace user fees it called excess charges—not recovered identity-theft proceeds, and not Ohio plus 30 other states. CMS under Biden had already taken anti-fraud steps against brokers in 2024; later Trump-era actions are additional, not the first discovery. [5][8][9]
    🔁 Rated misleading once before (Oct 3).
  6. Opinion
    “President Trump is the only president who listened and is giving overcharged taxpayers their money back, the way a successful business would.”
    This is praise and political framing, not a checkable factual claim.

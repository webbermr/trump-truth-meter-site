Claims checked

  2. False
    “The United States has the best employment numbers in history.”
    The latest BLS report (August 2026) showed unemployment at 4.3% and payroll growth of 22,000. That is weaker than recent lows: unemployment was 3.4% in January and April 2023 and 3.5% in 2019, and monthly job gains were far larger in 2021–2022 and parts of the late 2010s. A larger total employment level mainly reflects a bigger population, not a record labor market. [1][2][3]

  3. False
    “The Fake News Media refuses to report these employment numbers.”

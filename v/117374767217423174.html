Claims checked

  1. Mostly true
    “A new Council of Economic Advisers report says the agenda would cost Americans trillions of dollars.”
    The CEA released 'The Real Cost of Socialism in America' on Oct 2, 2026. It estimates about $49 trillion in new deficits over ten years from seven DSA platform policies. This is the administration's own partisan estimate, and Medicare for All makes up about $47.4 trillion of it. [1][2][3][4]
  2. Misleading
    “The 'Democrat Social Agenda' would cost Americans trillions if Democrats were elected to office.”
    The report scored the 'Workers Deserve More' program of the Democratic Socialists of America, not the Democratic Party's platform. The Democratic Party does not back positions like abolishing police and prisons or cancelling all student debt. Pinning the full cost on Democrats winning office overstates what the report shows. [1][2][6][7]
  3. False
    “The attached release says Trump added over one million jobs in under two years.”
    BLS payroll data show about 776,000 jobs added between January 2025 and September 2026, short of one million. I could not independently confirm the exact figure this was checked against. [8]
  4. Misleading
    “The attached release says manufacturing is surging and has added tens of thousands of jobs.”
    Manufacturing has recovered from its December 2025 low, but by September 2026 it was still slightly below its January 2025 level. [8]
  5. Opinion
    “America would be completely destroyed and become a Third World Country.”
    This is campaign rhetoric and a prediction, not a checkable fact.

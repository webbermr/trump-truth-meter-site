Claims checked

  1. True
    “Annie Andrews is running for the U.S. Senate in South Carolina.”
    Dr. Annie Andrews, a pediatrician, is the Democratic nominee for the 2026 South Carolina Senate seat. [1][2]
  2. False
    “Darline Graham is running against Annie Andrews for the Senate in South Carolina.”
    There is no Senate candidate named Darline Graham. The Republican nominee is incumbent Lindsey Graham, who won the June 9, 2026 GOP primary. Ballotpedia and major outlets list the general-election matchup as Graham vs. Andrews. [1][2][3]
  3. Unverifiable
    “Lindsey Graham has for years described Darline as the finest, most reliable, one of the smartest, and the most loyal person he has known.”
    No public record of Lindsey Graham using those words about a “Darline” was found. The anecdote also treats Darline as someone other than Graham himself, which does not match the race. [1][3]
  4. Misleading
    “Annie Andrews favors “Transgender for Everyone.””
    Andrews has defended gender-affirming care, including for minors, and LGBTQ rights. That is not a proposal that everyone should be transgender. [4][5]
  5. False
    “Annie Andrews supports open borders.”
    Her published immigration platform calls for border security alongside a path for people already here. It does not call for open borders. [6]
  6. False
    “Annie Andrews supports defunding the police.”
    In the South Carolina FOP’s 2026 Senate questionnaire she called for fair pay, benefits, training, and resources for officers, not defunding. [7]
  7. Misleading
    “Annie Andrews favors high taxes.”
    She proposes ending tax breaks for the ultra-wealthy and some corporations. “High taxes” as a blanket description leaves out whom the increases would target. [8]
  8. Misleading
    “Annie Andrews favors a weak military.”
    She argues for ending open-ended wars and requiring congressional approval for force, while supporting veterans and military families. That is not a stated preference for a weak military. [9]
  9. Misleading
    “Annie Andrews will fight to take voters’ guns away and is against the Second Amendment.”
    She supports background checks, safe storage, and limits on what she calls weapons of war, and she says those steps can coexist with the Second Amendment. The post turns proposed restrictions into confiscation and opposition to the amendment. [10]
  10. False
    “Trump has given Darline Graham his complete and total endorsement for this race.”
    Trump has endorsed Lindsey Graham for reelection. There is no candidate named Darline Graham to endorse, so this endorsement does not match the ballot. [2][3][11]

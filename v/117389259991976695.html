Claims checked

  1. False
    “Gasoline prices are no longer being driven up by the Strait of Hormuz.”
    As of early October 2026, Hormuz traffic was still far below normal, tankers were still being attacked, and analysts and the EIA still treat the disruption as a major reason oil and gasoline prices are high. [1][2][3]
    🔁 Rated misleading once before (Sep 29).
  2. False
    “Record numbers of barrels are coming out of the Strait of Hormuz on an almost daily basis.”
    September Gulf exports rose above pre-war levels mainly by avoiding the strait. Trackers still put Hormuz traffic about 75% below pre-conflict levels, not at daily records through the strait. [1][2][4]
  3. True
    “Ukraine is blowing up Russian refineries.”
    Ukraine has kept up drone strikes on Russian refineries, including Ryazan, and on Oct. 5 said those attacks had knocked out more than half of Russia's refining capacity. That figure is Ukraine's own claim, but the strikes themselves are well documented. [5][6]
  4. Misleading
    “U.S. refineries in blue states such as California are being closed by Democrats.”
    Phillips 66's Los Angeles-area refinery and Valero's Benicia plant are closing or have closed, about 17% of California capacity. Those were company decisions citing economics, demand, and long-term viability. State rules and costs are a factor, but Democrats did not order the plants shut. [7][8][9]

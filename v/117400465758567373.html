Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “Chris LaCivita and James Blair are outside consultants and do not work for Trump other than as outside consultants.”
    Neither currently holds a government job. Blair was White House deputy chief of staff for political strategy and outreach until about May 2026, when he left to run the GOP midterm effort; he and LaCivita now direct Trump’s political operation and large outside-money apparatus and are widely described as his top political advisers. “Outside consultant” is the formal status, but “they do not work for me” understates how closely they work for him. [1][2][3][4]
  2. Mostly true
    “They are allowed to do work for others, just like all consultants in Washington, D.C.”
    Private consultants who are not government employees may generally take other clients. That does not make every engagement conflict-free: paid political work for a foreign government or entity can trigger FARA disclosure, and the reported payments for Republika Srpska advice ran through a Washington law firm. Post-employment limits can also apply to someone who recently left a senior White House job. [1][5][6]
  3. Unverifiable
    “Trump does not know who else LaCivita and Blair work for.”
    This is a claim about his personal knowledge. Public reporting shows the two men have had other clients, including the reported Republika Srpska work and, in other coverage, crypto-related consulting, but no source establishes what Trump personally knew on October 7, 2026. [1][7]
  4. Opinion
    “He will ask them about other clients and terminate them immediately if there is a conflict.”
    This is a conditional promise about future action, not a checkable fact.

Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “Trump settled eight wars.”
    The administration counts a shifting set of ceasefires and diplomatic deals as wars ended. Several were limited clashes, unfinished, or disputed as U.S.-settled wars, not eight durably settled wars. [1][2]
  2. Unverifiable
    “He is close to ending or settling two more wars.”
    The post does not name the two conflicts, so proximity to settlement cannot be checked.
  3. Mostly true
    “He got all Israeli hostages back, including the final 28, living and dead.”
    By late January 2026 Israel said the remaining Gaza hostages, living and dead, had been returned and the hostage file was closed. The deal involved mediators and Hamas, not Trump alone. [3][4]
    🔁 Checked 3 times before.
  4. Misleading
    “Hundreds of hostages from countries all over the world were released and sent home.”
    About 251 people were taken from Israel on Oct. 7, 2023, including some foreign nationals, and later released or recovered. That is not a separate global operation freeing hundreds of hostages from countries worldwide. [5][6]
  5. Misleading
    “The United States won a war in Venezuela and captured the dictator who ran the country.”
    U.S. forces captured Nicolás Maduro in a January 3, 2026 raid and flew him to the United States. That was a capture operation, not a war the United States won or a settlement of Venezuelan rule. [7][8]
  6. Misleading
    “He stopped Iran, the leading state sponsor of terror, from obtaining a nuclear weapon.”
    The U.S. still lists Iran as a state sponsor of terrorism. 2025 strikes damaged nuclear sites and delayed a weapons path, but assessments say enriched material and know-how remain and the program was not permanently stopped. [9][10][11]
    🔁 Rated false 4 times before, since Sep 29.
  7. True
    “He, or the United States, did not receive the Nobel Peace Prize.”
    The 2026 Nobel Peace Prize went to María Corina Machado. Trump has not received it. The post misspells Nobel as Noble. [12][13]

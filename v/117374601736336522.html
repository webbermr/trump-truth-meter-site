Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “Recruiting for every branch has been record-setting ever since President Trump won in November 2024.”
    The services largely met or exceeded FY2025 accession goals, and several reported strong FY2026 results. That is not the same as every branch setting records continuously from the November 2024 election. The rebound was already underway in FY2024, when the Army, Navy, Air Force, and Space Force met goals after 2022–23 shortfalls, helped by programs such as the Army Future Soldier Preparatory Course that began under the prior administration. [1][2][3][4]
  2. Misleading
    “Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Air Force, Space Force, National Guard, Reserves, pilots, and Special Forces are all setting decades-long recruiting records.”
    Some active components posted their best recent years—the Army said it signed more than 61,000 recruits in FY2025, its strongest year in over a decade, and the Navy and Air Force also beat goals—but official releases do not show decades-long records for every item on this list. Space Force was established in 2019, so it cannot hold a decades-long record. Meeting a goal is also not the same as a historic accession high, and there is no public evidence that pilots or special-operations recruiting set decades-long records. Guard and Reserve results are mixed by component, not a single decades-long record. [1][2][5][6][7]
  3. Opinion
    “Americans want to serve when they are led by strong patriots, not weak weirdos.”
    Partisan characterization of leadership and of why people enlist, not a checkable fact.

Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “Trump canceled nearly $1 billion in congressionally appropriated funding.”
    On Sept. 25 he transmitted 11 proposed rescissions totaling $810 million and the administration withheld the money near the fiscal-year end. “Nearly $1B” is White House rounding of $810 million. GAO concluded the special message does not authorize withholding the funds past Sept. 30 without congressional action, so “canceled” treats a disputed pocket withholding as a finished cancellation. [1][2][3]
  2. Misleading
    “The funding was for illegal immigrants and race-focused programs.”
    The largest proposed cut, about $567 million, is HHS refugee and entrant assistance for refugees, asylees, and other eligible noncitizens, not a program reserved for people present illegally. Smaller lines do match White House descriptions of race- or DEI-related accounts (Minority Business Development Agency, Community Relations Service, Office of Minority Health) and some immigration-related items, but the package also includes international education, housing counseling, research, and other accounts. [1][2][4]
  3. True
    “The action was a new “pocket” rescission, described as his second.”
    The request was sent days before the Sept. 30 fiscal-year deadline so the Impoundment Control Act’s 45-day review window would not run—the standard definition of a pocket rescission—and reporting identifies it as the administration’s second such action this term. [1][2][3][5]

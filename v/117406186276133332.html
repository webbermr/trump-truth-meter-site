Claims checked

  1. Unverifiable
    “The US is having productive discussions with Iran.”
    Indirect talks through mediators are happening. But AP reports major hurdles remain, and October 7 reporting said talks had made little progress while the military was told to be ready for possible strikes. "Productive" is Trump's own description and can't be checked from outside. [1][2][3]
  2. Mostly true
    “Iran is in very bad condition economically and militarily.”
    The rial hit a record low, oil exports have dried up under the blockade, and US strikes have hit military targets. Iran can still threaten shipping, though, and "very bad" is partly a judgment call. [4][5][6]
  3. True
    “The blockade remains in full force and effect.”
    Reporting from early October describes the US blockade of Iranian ports and oil exports as still in effect. [1][4][6]
  4. False
    “Oil is flowing through Hormuz in record numbers, with 22 million barrels last night alone.”
    Kpler puts Middle East exports at about 22.4 million barrels per day. That is a daily total, not one night, and it includes fuels, chemicals and oil routed around Hormuz. CENTCOM cited about 20 million, and the EIA's pre-war Hormuz average was about 21 million a day. Flows have recovered to roughly pre-war levels, not hit a record. [7][8][9][10][11][12]
    🔁 Rated false once before (Oct 5).
  5. Mostly true
    “Not one barrel of that oil came from or went to Iran.”
    US officials, including Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent, say Iran has not loaded new export cargoes under the blockade, and independent tracking confirms its exports have collapsed. No one can confirm where every barrel on a single night came from or went. [4][5][13]
  6. True
    “The 2026 US midterm elections are on November 3.”
    The FEC lists November 3, 2026 as the general election date. [14]
    🔁 Checked once before.
  7. Opinion
    “The US will not attack Iran before the midterms, and Iran will not have a nuclear weapon.”
    These are promises about the future, not checkable facts. Trump had earlier refused to rule out strikes before the midterms, and recent reporting says the military was ordered to be ready.

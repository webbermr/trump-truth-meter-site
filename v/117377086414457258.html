Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “If Republicans win the House and Senate in the 2026 midterms, Trump will give every adult U.S. citizen $5,000.”
    He has repeatedly pledged a “Trump dividend” of $5,000 to adult citizens if Republicans keep both chambers. The election result alone cannot authorize the payments: Congress must appropriate the money. Analysts put the cost near $1.2 trillion for roughly 240 million to 270 million adults, and nothing has been enacted. [1][2][3]
    🔁 Rated misleading 3 times before, since Sep 26.
    • Also checked by professional fact-checkers:
    • Snopes: True on “U.S. President Donald Trump has promised $5,000 to every American adult if the Republicans win majority control of the Senate and House of Representatives in the 2026 midterm elections.”
  2. False
    “Republicans can fund the checks, and Democrats cannot, because of massive tariff revenue and record economic success.”
    Customs duties were about $195 billion in FY2025 and lower in FY2026 after refunds following the Supreme Court’s IEEPA tariff ruling. That is far short of a roughly $1.2 trillion one-time payout, and tariff revenue is already counted in deficit projections. [3][4][5]
  3. Mostly true
    “The Trump dividend would cost approximately $1 trillion.”
    Independent estimates are about $1.2 trillion to $1.3 trillion, depending on the adult-citizen count. “Approximately $1 trillion” is the right order of magnitude but a bit low. [1][2][3]
  4. False
    “A record-setting $21 trillion has been invested in the United States in the last 15 months.”
    Fact-checkers and the White House’s own tracker do not support $21 trillion already invested. The administration’s list of major investment announcements was around $9.6 trillion to $11 trillion and mixes long-term pledges and trade deals, not cash already invested or available for checks. New foreign direct investment in 2025 was about $232 billion. [6][7][8]
  5. Mostly true
    “He recently gave all members of the military $1,776, and everyone received the money.”
    In December 2025 about 1.45 million service members through pay grade O-6, plus eligible reservists, received a one-time $1,776 “Warrior Dividend,” and the payments went out. Flag officers were excluded, so it was not literally every member, and the money was a congressionally approved housing-allowance supplement, not a new tariff-funded program he created on his own. [9][10]
  6. Opinion
    “If Democrats win, they will raise taxes and cause a 1929-style economic depression.”
    This is a prediction and a partisan attack, not a checkable fact.

Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “Jack Smith’s office bent its own purchasing rules to rush a forensic video software purchase.”
    FOIA records released by the Oversight Project show executive officer Ariel McIntyre noting Smith preferred stricter-than-standard DOJ rules, initially objecting to skipping them, then authorizing an “urgent” Axon Investigate purchase before a UFMS obligation was created. The same authorization said the purchase was already approved and funds were certified. That is a documented procedural shortcut, not evidence of an unauthorized or illegal buy; “bent its own rules” is the outlet’s loaded framing. [1][2][3]
  2. Misleading
    “The purchase came days before Smith indicted Trump over the 2020 election.”
    The vendor order was submitted around July 14, 2023, and license dongles shipped about July 15. Smith’s 2020-election indictment was filed August 1, 2023 — roughly 17–18 days, or two weeks, later. Breitbart’s own article body says “just two weeks later,” which undercuts its “days before” headline. The records do not show what video the software was for. [1][4][5]

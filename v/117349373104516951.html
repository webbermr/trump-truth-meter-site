Claims checked

  1. Mostly true
    “Mesabi Metallics will build a $15 billion steel mill in Iowa.”
    The White House and the company are announcing a planned roughly $15 billion Iowa mill, to be supplied with iron ore from Mesabi's Minnesota mine. It is a proposal, not a finished plant: production is not expected before 2030, and the same Indian owner, Essar, saw an earlier Minnesota mine-and-mill effort stall and enter bankruptcy in 2016. [1][2][5][6]
  2. Misleading
    “This will become the largest steel plant in American history.”
    That wording is the White House's description, not an independent finding. The first phase is 7.5 million tons a year, which Reuters called one of the largest U.S. plants, not the largest. U.S. Steel's existing Gary Works already has about 7.5 to 8.2 million tons of capacity and is described as North America's largest integrated mill. A later expansion toward 10 million tons is a goal, and the plant has not been built. [1][2][4]
  3. Mostly true
    “The project will create thousands of jobs.”
    Officials project at least 1,750 permanent Iowa jobs and 5,000 to 6,000 construction jobs. Those figures support 'thousands,' but they are forecasts that depend on the project being financed and finished. [2][3][7]
  4. Opinion
    “The project will add billions to the economy for decades to come.”
    A long-run economic payoff is a forecast. The White House has projected about $95 billion in activity over construction and the first 10 years, but that is an administration estimate, not a demonstrated result. [3]
  5. Opinion
    “President Trump is prioritizing hardworking Americans instead of foreign labor.”
    This is political framing of intent, not a checkable fact about who will be hired. The announced job numbers do not show a choice between American and foreign workers. The investor is owned by India's Essar Group, which is foreign capital, not the same thing as foreign labor. [6]
  6. Opinion
    “American manufacturing is back.”
    Slogan-level praise, not a specific factual claim this proposed mill can establish.

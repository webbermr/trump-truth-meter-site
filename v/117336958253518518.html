Claims checked

  1. True
    “Trump had an election win.”
    He won the 2024 election by 312 electoral votes to 226. Whether the win was “big” is subjective.
  2. Misleading
    “Almost 100% of Trump coverage is negative and has been for years.”
    Coverage can be heavily negative, but the cited breadth and duration are unsupported. Pew’s analysis of early 2017 coverage found 56% of CNN stories and 81% of MSNBC stories negative; it does not establish a rate for every subsequent year.
  3. Misleading
    “White House access is a privilege that can be withheld from these outlets.”
    The article accurately quotes the White House’s position, and the outlets’ credentials were revoked. But on September 24 a federal judge ordered their temporary restoration, finding the outlets likely to succeed on a due-process claim. That was an interim order, not a final judgment.
  4. Opinion
    “CNN and “MSDNC” perpetrate fake news, and the White House is sacred.”
    These are broad characterizations and rhetoric, not specific factual allegations to score.

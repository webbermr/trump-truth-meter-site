Claims checked

  1. True

  2. Misleading
    “Trump’s pharmaceutical tariffs brought that campus to Ohio and have led to over $600 billion in new pharma investments.”
    The administration cites a large pile of multi-year company pledges, not verified new spending caused by tariffs. Bayer has described the Ohio site as part of a longer U.S. supply and growth plan, not as a tariff-driven decision. [1][2][3][4]
    🔁 Rated misleading 2 times before, since Sep 26.
  3. Misleading
    “Those policies have produced lower Most Favored Nation (MFN) drug prices.”
    The administration has announced MFN-style deals covering some drugs and programs, but that is not evidence of a broad drop in U.S. drug prices caused by pharmaceutical tariffs. [5][6]
    🔁 Rated false 2 times before, since Oct 1.

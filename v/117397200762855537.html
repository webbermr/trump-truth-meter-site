Claims checked

  1. Mostly true
    “Jack Smith’s team monitored a Trump staffer’s (Susie Wiles’s) phone communications while she ran the 2024 campaign.”
    Released FBI records show that, in the classified-documents probe under Special Counsel Jack Smith, investigators used a pen register and trap-and-trace device on Susie Wiles’s phone from about February to May 2023, while she was a senior Trump campaign official. Those tools log numbers, times, and duration, not the contents of calls or texts. “Spied on communications” implies interception of what was said, which the records do not show. [1][2][3]
  2. Misleading
    “Jack Smith spied on more than 20 lawyers.”
    The same Wiles phone logs include contacts with at least 21 Trump-affiliated lawyers. Those lawyers appeared because they called or messaged Wiles’s monitored phone. The records do not show separate surveillance of the lawyers’ phones or interception of their conversations. [1][2][3]
  3. Misleading
    “Investigators also spied on Wiles’s emails, as the linked article states.”
    Documents describe February 2023 preservation requests to Google, Microsoft, and other providers for Wiles’s email-account information. Reporting of those records describes preservation of account data or metadata, not collection or reading of email contents. [2][3]

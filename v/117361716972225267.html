MisleadingAccuracy 40/100

The post accurately quotes the Fed inspector general on late cost estimates, no guaranteed maximum price, and management deficiencies, and the roughly $2.5 billion cost is in the right range. It leaves out the report’s finding of no crime and no administrative misconduct, treats former chair Powell as if he still runs interest-rate policy, and adds unsupported claims that the project has no end, destroyed historic finishes, and is hundreds of millions over a $2.5 billion budget.

Editor’s note: All three reviewers agreed the quoted IG findings on late estimates, no GMP, and management deficiencies are real, and that the post is an attack in Trump’s own voice. They split on Blanche’s title (Grok wrongly called him Deputy AG; he is Attorney General), on whether Powell still runs rate policy (Grok missed that Warsh has been chair since May 2026), and on whether the “destroyed historic finishes” line is unverifiable or false. I followed the OIG and Fed sources: the quotes are accurate, the no-misconduct finding and Powell’s status are crucial omissions, and the beauty claim conflicts with the project’s documented restoration work.

Claims checked

  1. True
    “An Inspector General report found deficiencies in the management of the Federal Reserve headquarters renovation.”
    The Fed OIG’s September 2026 evaluation found deficiencies in how the Board managed the Eccles and 1951 Constitution Avenue renovation, including weak cost controls. [1][2]
  2. True
    “The report says the Board did not obtain a construction-manager project cost estimate until January 2026, 3.5 years after construction began and after more than $2 billion in construction costs had been awarded.”
    That language is in the OIG report essentially as quoted. Construction began in 2022; the first formal construction-manager estimate came in January 2026 after more than $2 billion had been awarded. [1][2]
  3. True
    “As of July 2026, more than four years after construction began and after $2 billion had been awarded, the Board still had not set a guaranteed maximum price, so it lacked cost certainty and had not transferred material risk to the construction manager.”
    The OIG report includes this finding nearly verbatim. [1][2]
  4. Misleading
    “The complex is hundreds of millions of dollars over budget, over budget at a record-setting rate, and will cost at least $2.5 billion according to the report, with no end in sight.”
    The approved project budget rose from about $1.9 billion (earlier figures near $1.3–1.9 billion depending on what is counted) to roughly $2.4–2.5 billion, so a large overrun and a ~$2.5 billion total are real. The OIG did not call the overrun record-setting, did not say the project is hundreds of millions over a $2.5 billion budget, and gave a planned construction completion of December 2027 rather than no end in sight. [1][2][3]
  5. Opinion
    “The final cost will actually be $3.5 billion or maybe $4 billion, and the building may never be properly built.”
    Trump presents these higher totals and the claim that it may never be finished properly as his own prediction, not as findings in the report.
  6. False
    “Soundproofing and other elements will be inferior, and sculptured ceilings, walls, and other historic finishes are being replaced by flat, boring finishes, destroying the building’s beauty and history.”
    The IG report is about cost management and oversight, not a finding that finishes or soundproofing will be inferior. Project documentation describes preservation and reuse of historic materials, including a large share of restored marble, not a documented destruction of the building’s historic character. [1][3]
  7. Opinion
    “Trump could have done a far better renovation for $25 million while fully maintaining the building’s magnificence.”
    This is an unsupported personal boast, not a documented alternative bid for the two-building modernization.
  8. Misleading
    “Powell manages the Fed’s high interest-rate policy and should be forced to resign immediately; if he does not, the government should sue him for corruption or incompetence.”
    Powell’s chair term ended in May 2026; Kevin Warsh is chair, and rate decisions are made by the FOMC, not by Powell alone. The IG found management deficiencies but no reasonable grounds for a criminal referral and no administrative misconduct. The demand that he resign or be sued is political opinion built on that omission. [1][2][4][5]
  9. Mostly true
    “Trump has asked Attorney General Todd Blanche to study the report and decide what to do.”
    Todd Blanche is Attorney General as of 2026, not Deputy Attorney General. The request itself is Trump’s own statement and is not independently confirmed in public records, but the title he uses is correct. [6]
  10. Opinion
    “Trump does not want the Federal Reserve building named after him.”
    This is a statement of intent, not a checkable external fact.

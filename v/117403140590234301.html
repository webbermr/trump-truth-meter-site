Claims checked

    “James Talarico is running for the United States Senate in Texas.”
    Talarico, a Texas state representative, is the Democratic nominee in the 2026 U.S. Senate race against Republican Ken Paxton. [1][2]
  2. Misleading
    “Talarico is missing in action and no one has seen him for an extended period of time.”
    Republicans, including Paxton, pointed to an absence of about 11 days from in-person campaigning. His campaign said he had the flu and had canceled events, and he appeared virtually around Sept. 30. Calling him missing and unseen omits that publicly reported explanation. [3][4][5]

    “Bad rumors are coming out about what is wrong with him.”
    The post names no rumor. The only publicly reported reason for the pause was a flu diagnosis; no major outlet confirmed a separate scandal matching the innuendo. [3][4][5]
  4. Misleading
    “Ken Paxton is ready, willing, and able to debate Talarico, who should report to a debate stage.”
    Reporting shows Talarico accepted multiple debate invitations, including CNN’s, and challenged Paxton to a Fox News debate that Fox agreed to host. As of early October, Paxton had not accepted those invitations and his campaign had gone quiet on debates. [1][6][7][8]
  5. Opinion
    “Ken Paxton is one of the best attorneys general in the country and will soon, hopefully, be a U.S. senator from Texas.”
    This is praise and a hope about an election that has not happened, not a checkable fact.

Claims checked

  1. True
    “swampthevoteusa.com tells people how and where to vote.”
    The site is a real Republican get-out-the-vote page with state voting options for early, mail, absentee, and Election Day voting. It is a campaign site, not an official election office. [1][2]
    🔁 Checked once before.
  2. True
    “Election Day for the 2026 midterms is November 3.”
    Federal law sets congressional elections for the Tuesday after the first Monday in November. In 2026 that date is November 3, and the FEC lists it as the next federal general election. [3][4]
  3. False
    “Whenever Trump is on the ballot, Republicans get 45% more votes.”
    No turnout or vote-total series shows a 45% premium when Trump is on the ballot. Census figures put citizen turnout at about 65.3% in 2024 versus 52.2% in 2022 — roughly 25% higher, not 45% — and that gap is the usual presidential-versus-midterm pattern, not a Trump-specific effect. Republican House vote totals also do not rise by 45% in his presidential years. [5][6][7]
    🔁 Rated false once before (Oct 3).
  4. Misleading
    “Trump gave the biggest tax cut in history.”
    He signed large cuts, including the 2017 Tax Cuts and Jobs Act and the 2025 tax law. Measured against the economy, neither is the largest in U.S. history: the Tax Foundation ranks the 2025 law about sixth and the 2017 law about eleventh among cuts since 1940, behind several earlier packages including Reagan’s 1981 cut. [8][9][10]
    🔁 Rated misleading once before (Oct 3).

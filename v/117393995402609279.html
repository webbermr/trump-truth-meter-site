Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “Trump’s rally crowds and enthusiasm are unprecedented; nobody has ever seen anything like them.”
    Recent stops have drawn enthusiastic crowds, including a reported sellout of about 5,800 in Grand Island, Nebraska. They are not historic: venues are far smaller than his 2015–16 tours, and reporters described empty upper sections at the Oct. 2 Mobile, Alabama rally and at a recent Ohio rally. [1][2][3]
  2. False
    “Republicans are flipping races all over the place.”
    The midterms have not been held. Cook Political Report’s September House overview shifted 15 ratings toward Democrats and said the GOP was on track to lose the House majority. Democrats lead generic-ballot averages, and several Republican-held Senate seats have moved toward Democrats, not a broad GOP flip wave. [4][5][6]
  3. Opinion
    “The Fake News knows this but will not tell viewers the truth.”
    A media-motive attack, not a checkable fact.
  4. Unverifiable
    “Networks covering the rallies in full are being rewarded with big ratings.”
    At least one local outlet, News Channel Nebraska, said it would air the Grand Island rally. No public ratings figures show that full-rally coverage is producing unusually large audiences, and the post names no network or comparison. [7]
  5. Opinion
    “Democrats never counted on this, and a “Midterm Surprise” is coming.”
    Political prediction and rhetoric, not a present factual claim.

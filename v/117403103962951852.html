Claims checked

  1. False
    “The Democratic Senate candidate opposing Mike Rogers is named Mohammed El-Sayed.”
    The Democratic nominee is Abdul El-Sayed (full name Abdulrahman Mohamed El-Sayed). He goes by Abdul, not Mohammed; Mohamed is his middle name and his father’s name. He is running against Republican Mike Rogers for Michigan’s open U.S. Senate seat. [1][2][3]
  2. False
    “El-Sayed “goes back to the days of the Jihad” and is a radical extremist and terrorist sympathizer.”
    No credible evidence links El-Sayed to jihad, the Muslim Brotherhood, or terrorist groups. Fact-checkers found false the claim that he said America deserved 9/11; that was a 2019 Hasan Piker remark El-Sayed called “dumb.” He condemned the March 2026 Temple Israel attack; a later “hurt people hurt people” line, which critics said sounded like justification, he apologized for and said was not meant to excuse terrorism. [4][5][6][7]
  3. False
    “Hasan Piker is El-Sayed’s best friend, and El-Sayed is no different from him.”
    Piker endorsed El-Sayed and appeared with him in the primary, but they are not documented best friends. El-Sayed has said he disagrees with Piker on many things, disavowed Piker’s 9/11 comment, said he had no plans to campaign with him in the general election, and was reported to have privately asked him to tone down his rhetoric. “America hating” is an opinion about Piker, not a fact that equates the two. [8][9][10][11]
  4. True
    “Mike Rogers has Trump’s complete and total endorsement.”
    Trump endorsed Rogers for the Michigan Senate seat before this post, using the same “complete and total endorsement” line, and restates it here. Later policy disagreements by Rogers do not withdraw the endorsement. [12][13]
  5. Opinion
    “El-Sayed is a terrible candidate whose values are not those of Michiganders or the United States, and Rogers is extremely good and will never let voters down.”
    Campaign praise, insult, and prediction. “How’s France doing?” is a rhetorical jab, not a checkable claim about El-Sayed.

Claims checked

  1. False
    “Fake News reports regarding the ongoing investigation into the Fed renovation are total lies.”
    The main reports said the IG found no grounds for a criminal referral and that DOJ won't reopen its criminal probe. Both match the IG report and Blanche's own interview. No DOJ investigation is ongoing. [1][2][3][4][5]
  2. Mostly true
    “Newly confirmed Fed Chairman Kevin Warsh has announced an in-depth, independent audit.”
    Warsh's Sept. 29 response to the IG commits the Fed to hiring an independent auditor to check costs, and he moved project control to GSA. He was sworn in in May 2026, so 'newly' is a slight stretch. [2][6][7][8]
  3. Misleading
    “Attorney General Todd Blanche clearly stated the Justice Department will investigate any and all evidence of wrongdoing.”
    Blanche's headline statement was that DOJ is 'not reopening a criminal investigation' into Powell, and he said he was satisfied with the IG report. He only said DOJ could act if the audit turns up evidence of crimes. That conditional remark is not a pledge to investigate. [1][4]
    🔁 Checked once before.
  4. True
    “The IG report detailed that the project is Hundreds of Millions of Dollars over budget and way behind schedule.”
    The budget rose from about $1.3 billion in 2020 to about $2.4 billion, which is actually more than $1 billion over. Planned completion slipped from 2024 to December 2027. The report found deficient project management. [2][3][7]
    🔁 Checked once before.
  5. Misleading
    “The overruns and delays are due to Powell's total lack of leadership, oversight and competence.”
    The IG blamed Board-level project management and also cited inflation and design changes. It said staff ran day-to-day management and found no administrative misconduct. It did not pin the failure on Powell personally, though Blanche separately said Powell's oversight was inadequate. [1][3][7]
  6. Opinion
    “I look forward to learning the results of the professional audit and the DOJ investigation.”
    This expresses a hope. Note that no DOJ investigation is currently open.

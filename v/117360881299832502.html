Claims checked

  1. Mostly true
    “Fox News features Chuck Schumer, Hakeem Jeffries, Jessica Tarlov, and other Democrats criticizing Republicans and Trump.”
    Jessica Tarlov is a regular liberal co-host on Fox’s The Five, and Fox has aired clips and interviews with Schumer and Jeffries. That does not show they are on “always,” or that their appearances outweigh the network’s conservative lineup. [4][5][6]
  2. Unverifiable
    “Democrats get more Fox News airtime than pro-Republican voices, who only get a quick retort.”
    The post presents this as what he “actually think[s],” with no period or airtime figures. Fox’s opinion programming is still built around conservative hosts; no public comparison supports a network-wide Democratic airtime majority. [4]
  3. False
    “Trump won all three of his presidential elections.”
    He won the Electoral College in 2016 (304–227) and 2024 (312–226). In 2020 Joe Biden won 306–232; Congress certified that result and Biden was inaugurated. An earlier finding that he had an election win, or won Missouri in all three races, is narrower and does not make this claim true. [1][2][3]

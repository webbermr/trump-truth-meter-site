Claims checked

  1. Mostly true
    “Fox News regularly puts on Democrats such as Chuck Schumer, Hakeem Jeffries, and Jessica Tarlov who criticize Republicans and Trump.”
    Jessica Tarlov is a longtime liberal co-host on Fox’s The Five, and Fox has aired clips and appearances featuring Schumer and other Democrats criticizing Trump. That does not show Fox “always” books them or every Democrat. [1][2]
  2. Misleading
    “Those Democratic voices get more Fox News airtime than pro-Republican voices, who receive only a quick retort.”
    No network-wide airtime count supports that comparison. Fox’s flagship panel shows pair one rotating liberal co-host with several conservative co-hosts, and Trump officials appeared hundreds of times on Fox networks in the first 100 days of his second term. The “more airtime” line is an unproven impression, hedged with “I actually think.” [1][3]
  3. Opinion
    “MAGA and real patriots will never like Fox because it repeats a negative narrative.”
    Audience prediction and editorial characterization, not a checkable fact.
  4. False
    “Trump won all three of his presidential elections.”
    He won in 2016 and 2024. In 2020 Joe Biden won 306 electoral votes to Trump’s 232; the results were certified and Biden was inaugurated. Winning a state such as Missouri in all three cycles is not the same as winning all three national elections. Earlier claim [1] only says he had an election win; [2] is Missouri-only. [4][5][6]

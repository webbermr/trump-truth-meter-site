Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “If Republicans win the House and Senate in the 2026 midterms, Trump will give every adult U.S. citizen $5,000.”
    This is a repeated campaign pledge, not enacted law. Only Congress can appropriate the money; an election result would not authorize the president to issue the checks, and no funding bill has passed. [1][2][3]
    🔁 Rated misleading 4 times before, since Sep 26.
    • Also checked by professional fact-checkers:
    • Snopes.com: True on “U.S. President Donald Trump has promised $5,000 to every American adult if the Republicans win majority control of the Senate and House of Representatives in the 2026 midterm elections.”
  2. Mostly true
    “The proposed $5,000 Trump dividend would cost approximately $1 trillion.”
    About 230–260 million adult citizens times $5,000 is roughly $1.1–$1.3 trillion before administration, so 'approximately $1 trillion' is in the right range but a bit low. [2][4]
    🔁 Checked once before.
  3. False
    “A record-setting $21 trillion has been invested in the United States in the last 15 months.”
    No official series shows $21 trillion of completed investment in 15 months. Annual gross private domestic investment is on the order of $5 trillion, and White House tallies of announced pledges are far below $21 trillion and are not the same as money already invested. [5][6][7]
    🔁 Rated false once before (Oct 3).
  4. False
    “Republicans can fund the checks, and Democrats cannot, because of massive tariff revenue and record economic success.”
    Customs duties have risen under the 2025–26 tariffs but remain on the order of roughly $200–300 billion a year, a fraction of the ~$1 trillion-plus cost of one round of checks. Either party would need new legislation; tariff receipts do not uniquely enable Republicans to pay for this. [2][8][9]
    🔁 Rated false once before (Oct 3).
  5. Mostly true
    “Trump recently gave all members of the military $1,776, and everyone received the money.”
    About 1.45 million eligible service members received a one-time $1,776 'Warrior Dividend' in December 2025, paid from already appropriated housing-allowance funds. It was not a personal gift, and some ranks and categories were excluded. [10][11]
    🔁 Checked once before.
  6. Opinion
    “If Democrats win, there will be a 1929-style depression, taxes will rise, and the economy will be destroyed; a Republican win will bring economic success like never before.”
    These are campaign predictions and partisan attacks, not checkable facts.

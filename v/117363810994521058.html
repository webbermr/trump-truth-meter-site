Claims checked

  1. Misleading
    “A Boeing 737 Max withstood G-forces and structural strain far beyond what it was designed and expected to do.”
    Flydubai FZ1073, a 737 MAX 8 (A6-FKF), lost about 14,125 feet in 29 seconds after a flight-deck altercation, then was secured by other crew and landed at Tabuk with passengers safe. That is an extreme event outside normal operations, and secondary reports say speed exceeded the maximum operating limit. It does not show loads “far beyond” design: the UAE investigation is still open and has released no G-load data; transport-category rules already require the structure to carry 1.5 times limit load without failure; and images and reporting show a large section of the rudder was ripped off, so the airframe did not fully withstand the strain.   3. Opinion
    “Boeing builds, by far, the finest commercial airliners in the sky.”
    “By far the finest” is a superlative, not a measurable claim.
  4. True

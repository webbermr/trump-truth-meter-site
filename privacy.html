Trump Truth MeterAI fact-checks of Truth Social posts

Privacy policy

Last updated September 29, 2026

Trump Truth Meter is a personal fact-checking tool run by one person. It rates public posts on Truth Social with AI models and publishes the results on this site, as replies on Truth Social and as videos on its own YouTube channel.

What it uses

  • Public posts. It reads public Truth Social posts and replies to fact-check them. It doesn’t collect information about the people who read this site or watch its videos.
  • YouTube. When the owner connects the tool’s YouTube channel, Google gives it permission to upload videos to that channel and read the channel’s name. That permission is stored only on the owner’s own computer and is used only to upload the fact-check videos it makes. It isn’t used for anything else, shared, or sold.
  • This site has no cookies, analytics or tracking. It’s hosted by GitHub Pages, which may keep standard server logs (see GitHub’s privacy statement).

Sharing

No personal information is sold or shared with anyone. Post text is sent to AI providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, xAI and Google) to be fact-checked.

Removing access

The YouTube permission can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. The tool’s use of Google data follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, and YouTube uploads follow the YouTube Terms of Service.

Contact

Questions: open an issue on this site’s GitHub page.
